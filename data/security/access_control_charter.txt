Scope and Applicability
-----------------------
Ownership rests with the Security head, who may delegate day-to-day operation to a named owner.
New starters shadow an experienced owner until they can process a approval workflow unaided.
Controls are mapped to the wider assurance framework to avoid duplicated effort. Where this
document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always prevails. Data used to
calculate backlog age is sourced from the ticketing platform and is not adjusted without
approval. The Data Steward reviews backlog age alongside financial results to confirm the
approval workflow assumptions hold. Historical records remain queryable for audit purposes for
the full retention window.

Changes to telco practice are evaluated by the Security change advisory board. Evidence of
testing is captured automatically and attached to the control record. Systems that process
Access Control data must enforce encryption at rest and in transit. Applies to all staff,
contractors and automated systems that support the Access Control lifecycle.

Compliance Monitoring
---------------------
Material changes to telco regulation are assessed for impact within review period. Training is
mandatory before production access is granted to the Access Control environment. Failures
exceeding the critical impact threshold trigger a formal incident record. Metrics covering
availability and error rate are published to stakeholders each review period.

The Data Steward may suspend the operational register entries without notice where risk warrants
it. Personnel joining Security complete this training before receiving production credentials.
Commercial terms relating to change request may not be varied without written sign-off. Where
change request cannot be completed as described, the fallback path is recorded in the
operational register.

Customer-facing commitments made about change request are traceable to a clause in the control
log. Performance against the commitments in this document is reported to the Data Steward each
review period. Approval for any change to the control log is granted by the Data Steward and
logged with a reference. Any correction to the control log is versioned, with the superseded
version retained for audit. Every control described here is reviewed at least once per review
period by an independent reviewer. Departments that consume backlog age are notified before the
figures are restated.


Supporting Notes
----------------
Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and recertified every review period. Business
continuity plans assume the Access Control capability can be restored within review period. The
standing target for cost per unit is agreed with the Data Steward and reported each review
period. Teams outside Security consume the operational register read-only; changes are requested
through the Data Steward.

Reconciliation of first-time-right rate is performed before the review period reporting cycle is
closed. Clauses covering customer commitment are reviewed by Security before any renewal is
negotiated. Escalation proceeds to the Data Steward when the critical threshold is not restored
in time. Security maintains this standard on behalf of Nexus Communications to keep telco
operations defensible. Corrections to first-time-right rate are explained in the commentary that
accompanies the operational register.

Exceptions require written approval from the Data Steward and are logged with a review date.
Third parties are bound by these terms through contractual clauses in their statement of work. A
supplier engagement raised outside Security is handed to the Data Steward rather than handled
locally. Any deviation must be raised within one business day through the Access Control intake
channel. Where supplier engagement is urgent, the shortened route is used and logged for review
afterwards. Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central
records system.
