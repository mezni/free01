Compliance Monitoring
---------------------
New starters shadow an experienced owner until they can process a supplier engagement unaided.
Any deviation must be raised within one business day through the Risk Management intake channel.
Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and recertified every month. Disputes about
supplier engagement are settled by reference to the process record and the recorded decision.
Ownership rests with the Network Operations head, who may delegate day-to-day operation to a
named owner. Changes to telco practice are evaluated by the Network Operations change advisory
board.

Escalation proceeds to the Service Owner when the high threshold is not restored in time. A
operational task raised outside Network Operations is handed to the Service Owner rather than
handled locally. Reconciliation of throughput is performed before the month reporting cycle is
closed. Where this document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always
prevails. Material changes to telco regulation are assessed for impact within month.

Performance against the commitments in this document is reported to the Service Owner each
month. Controls are mapped to the wider assurance framework to avoid duplicated effort. Clauses
covering supplier engagement are reviewed by Network Operations before any renewal is
negotiated. Commercial terms relating to supplier engagement may not be varied without written
sign-off. Capacity to absorb cost per unit is reassessed every month and signed off by the
Service Owner. Data used to calculate cost per unit is sourced from the asset inventory and is
not adjusted without approval. Metrics covering availability and error rate are published to
stakeholders each month.
