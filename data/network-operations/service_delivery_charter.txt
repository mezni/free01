Purpose and Objectives
----------------------
Applies to all staff, contractors and automated systems that support the Service Delivery
lifecycle. Systems that process Service Delivery data must enforce encryption at rest and in
transit. Templates for supplier engagement are versioned centrally so that Network Operations
does not maintain local copies. Third parties are bound by these terms through contractual
clauses in their statement of work. Controls are mapped to the wider assurance framework to
avoid duplicated effort.

Disputes about operational task are settled by reference to the process record and the recorded
decision. Reconciliation of throughput is performed before the month reporting cycle is closed.
Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and recertified every month. Personnel joining
Network Operations complete this training before receiving production credentials.

Standard Operating Procedure
----------------------------
The standing target for first-time-right rate is agreed with the Process Manager and reported
each month. Capacity to absorb first-time-right rate is reassessed every month and signed off by
the Process Manager. A approval workflow raised outside Network Operations is handed to the
Process Manager rather than handled locally. New starters shadow an experienced owner until they
can process a approval workflow unaided. Training is mandatory before production access is
granted to the Service Delivery environment. Customer-facing commitments made about approval
workflow are traceable to a clause in the process record. Network Operations maintains this
standard on behalf of Nexus Communications to keep telco operations defensible.

Corrections to cost per unit are explained in the commentary that accompanies the operational
register. Changes to telco practice are evaluated by the Network Operations change advisory
board. Where customer commitment is urgent, the shortened route is used and logged for review
afterwards. Approval for any change to the operational register is granted by the Process
Manager and logged with a reference. Departments that consume cost per unit are notified before
the figures are restated.

Where this document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always prevails. The
Process Manager is accountable for confirming that this document is followed in practice.
Ownership rests with the Network Operations head, who may delegate day-to-day operation to a
named owner. Data used to calculate first-time-right rate is sourced from the change management
log and is not adjusted without approval. Where supplier engagement cannot be completed as
described, the fallback path is recorded in the process record. Business continuity plans assume
the Service Delivery capability can be restored within month.
