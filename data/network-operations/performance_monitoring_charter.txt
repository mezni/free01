Purpose and Objectives
----------------------
Approval for any change to the control log is granted by the Compliance Officer and logged with
a reference. Where supplier engagement is urgent, the shortened route is used and logged for
review afterwards. Where supplier engagement cannot be completed as described, the fallback path
is recorded in the control log. Capacity to absorb first-time-right rate is reassessed every
year and signed off by the Compliance Officer. Historical records remain queryable for audit
purposes for the full retention window. Third parties are bound by these terms through
contractual clauses in their statement of work.

Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and recertified every year. Evidence of testing is
captured automatically and attached to the control record. Reconciliation of backlog age is
performed before the year reporting cycle is closed. The standing target for backlog age is
agreed with the Compliance Officer and reported each year. The Compliance Officer reviews
backlog age alongside financial results to confirm the supplier engagement assumptions hold. The
Compliance Officer is accountable for confirming that this document is followed in practice.


Monitoring and Reporting
------------------------
Escalation proceeds to the Compliance Officer when the P1 threshold is not restored in time.
Clauses covering operational task are reviewed by Network Operations before any renewal is
negotiated. New starters shadow an experienced owner until they can process a operational task
unaided. Departments that consume first-time-right rate are notified before the figures are
restated. Customer-facing commitments made about operational task are traceable to a clause in
the process record. Commercial terms relating to operational task may not be varied without
written sign-off.

Controls are mapped to the wider assurance framework to avoid duplicated effort. Each supplier
engagement must be recorded in the operational register before it is presented to a customer.
Any correction to the operational register is versioned, with the superseded version retained
for audit. The Compliance Officer may suspend the operational register entries without notice
where risk warrants it.

Every control described here is reviewed at least once per year by an independent reviewer.
Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central records system.
Exceptions require written approval from the Compliance Officer and are logged with a review
date. Where this document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always prevails.
Data used to calculate first-time-right rate is sourced from the ticketing platform and is not
adjusted without approval.


Escalation and Incident Handling
--------------------------------
Business continuity plans assume the Performance Monitoring capability can be restored within
year. Personnel joining Network Operations complete this training before receiving production
credentials. Disputes about service request are settled by reference to the control log and the
recorded decision. Training is mandatory before production access is granted to the Performance
Monitoring environment. Network Operations maintains this standard on behalf of Nexus
Communications to keep telco operations defensible. Templates for service request are versioned
centrally so that Network Operations does not maintain local copies. Material changes to telco
regulation are assessed for impact within year.

Systems that process Performance Monitoring data must enforce encryption at rest and in transit.
A approval workflow raised outside Network Operations is handed to the Compliance Officer rather
than handled locally. Any deviation must be raised within one business day through the
Performance Monitoring intake channel. Metrics covering availability and error rate are
published to stakeholders each year. Teams outside Network Operations consume the process record
read-only; changes are requested through the Compliance Officer. Applies to all staff,
contractors and automated systems that support the Performance Monitoring lifecycle. Automated
checks run continuously and alert the on-call engineer when thresholds are breached.
