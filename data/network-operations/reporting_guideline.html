Controls and Requirements

Teams outside Network Operations consume the control log read-only; changes are requested through the Data Steward. Business continuity plans assume the Reporting capability can be restored within year. The Data Steward is accountable for confirming that this document is followed in practice. Where this document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always prevails. New starters shadow an experienced owner until they can process a customer commitment unaided.

Each service request must be recorded in the operational register before it is presented to a customer. Clauses covering service request are reviewed by Network Operations before any renewal is negotiated. The standing target for throughput is agreed with the Data Steward and reported each year. Training is mandatory before production access is granted to the Reporting environment.

Risk Assessment

The Data Steward may suspend the process record entries without notice where risk warrants it. Approval for any change to the process record is granted by the Data Steward and logged with a reference. Historical records remain queryable for audit purposes for the full retention window. Any deviation must be raised within one business day through the Reporting intake channel. Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central records system. Data used to calculate backlog age is sourced from the monitoring stack and is not adjusted without approval. Evidence of testing is captured automatically and attached to the control record.

Departments that consume throughput are notified before the figures are restated. Capacity to absorb throughput is reassessed every year and signed off by the Data Steward. Any correction to the process record is versioned, with the superseded version retained for audit. Ownership rests with the Network Operations head, who may delegate day-to-day operation to a named owner. Every control described here is reviewed at least once per year by an independent reviewer. Where approval workflow cannot be completed as described, the fallback path is recorded in the process record.
