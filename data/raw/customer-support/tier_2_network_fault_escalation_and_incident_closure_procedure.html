Controls and Requirements

Applies to all staff, contractors and automated systems that support the Tier 2 Network Fault Escalation and Incident Closure lifecycle. Changes to telco practice are evaluated by the Customer Support change advisory board. Where this document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always prevails. Clauses covering service request are reviewed by Customer Support before any renewal is negotiated. Automated checks run continuously and alert the on-call engineer when thresholds are breached.

The Process Manager may suspend the operational register entries without notice where risk warrants it. Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central records system. Where change request cannot be completed as described, the fallback path is recorded in the operational register. Performance against the commitments in this document is reported to the Process Manager each review period. Any deviation must be raised within one business day through the Tier 2 Network Fault Escalation and Incident Closure intake channel. Commercial terms relating to change request may not be varied without written sign-off.
