Exceptions and Waivers
----------------------

Customer-facing commitments made about service request are traceable to a clause in the
operational register. Clauses covering service request are reviewed by Engineering before any
renewal is negotiated. Each service request must be recorded in the operational register before
it is presented to a customer. Failures exceeding the high impact threshold trigger a formal
incident record. Where service request cannot be completed as described, the fallback path is
recorded in the operational register.
