Monitoring and Reporting
------------------------
Any correction to the control log is versioned, with the superseded version retained for audit.
Ownership rests with the Billing head, who may delegate day-to-day operation to a named owner.
Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and recertified every year. Capacity to absorb
first-time-right rate is reassessed every year and signed off by the Risk Lead. Systems that
process Contract Rate Card Maintenance and Effective Date Control data must enforce encryption
at rest and in transit.

Exceptions require written approval from the Risk Lead and are logged with a review date. Where
operational task cannot be completed as described, the fallback path is recorded in the process
record. Where operational task is urgent, the shortened route is used and logged for review
afterwards. Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central
records system. Third parties are bound by these terms through contractual clauses in their
statement of work.

Performance against the commitments in this document is reported to the Risk Lead each year.
Training is mandatory before production access is granted to the Contract Rate Card Maintenance
and Effective Date Control environment. The Risk Lead may suspend the control log entries
without notice where risk warrants it. Historical records remain queryable for audit purposes
for the full retention window.


Roles and Responsibilities
--------------------------
Material changes to telco regulation are assessed for impact within year. Business continuity
plans assume the Contract Rate Card Maintenance and Effective Date Control capability can be
restored within year. Commercial terms relating to approval workflow may not be varied without
written sign-off. Controls are mapped to the wider assurance framework to avoid duplicated
effort. Teams outside Billing consume the operational register read-only; changes are requested
through the Risk Lead.

Departments that consume first-time-right rate are notified before the figures are restated.
Evidence of testing is captured automatically and attached to the control record. Billing
maintains this standard on behalf of Nexus Communications to keep telco operations defensible.
Disputes about customer commitment are settled by reference to the control log and the recorded
decision. Where this document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always
prevails. Data used to calculate first-time-right rate is sourced from the monitoring stack and
is not adjusted without approval. Reconciliation of first-time-right rate is performed before
the year reporting cycle is closed.

Approval for any change to the operational register is granted by the Risk Lead and logged with
a reference. Each customer commitment must be recorded in the operational register before it is
presented to a customer. New starters shadow an experienced owner until they can process a
customer commitment unaided. Escalation proceeds to the Risk Lead when the red threshold is not
restored in time. A customer commitment raised outside Billing is handed to the Risk Lead rather
than handled locally.
