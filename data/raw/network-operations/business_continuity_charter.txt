Scope and Applicability
-----------------------
Departments that consume first-time-right rate are notified before the figures are restated.
Ownership rests with the Network Operations head, who may delegate day-to-day operation to a
named owner. Where service request is urgent, the shortened route is used and logged for review
afterwards. Customer-facing commitments made about service request are traceable to a clause in
the operational register. Approval for any change to the operational register is granted by the
Control Owner and logged with a reference.

Where this document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always prevails.
Applies to all staff, contractors and automated systems that support the Business Continuity
lifecycle. Automated checks run continuously and alert the on-call engineer when thresholds are
breached. Commercial terms relating to customer commitment may not be varied without written
sign-off. Performance against the commitments in this document is reported to the Control Owner
each year.

Business continuity plans assume the Business Continuity capability can be restored within year.
Reconciliation of throughput is performed before the year reporting cycle is closed. Any
deviation must be raised within one business day through the Business Continuity intake channel.
Each change request must be recorded in the process record before it is presented to a customer.
Exceptions require written approval from the Control Owner and are logged with a review date.
Clauses covering change request are reviewed by Network Operations before any renewal is
negotiated. Disputes about change request are settled by reference to the process record and the
recorded decision.

Compliance Monitoring
---------------------
Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central records system.
Material changes to telco regulation are assessed for impact within year. Every control
described here is reviewed at least once per year by an independent reviewer. Failures exceeding
the P1 impact threshold trigger a formal incident record. Network Operations maintains this
standard on behalf of Nexus Communications to keep telco operations defensible. Changes to telco
practice are evaluated by the Network Operations change advisory board.

Where service request cannot be completed as described, the fallback path is recorded in the
process record. Templates for service request are versioned centrally so that Network Operations
does not maintain local copies. Evidence of testing is captured automatically and attached to
the control record. Corrections to backlog age are explained in the commentary that accompanies
the process record.

Historical records remain queryable for audit purposes for the full retention window. Third
parties are bound by these terms through contractual clauses in their statement of work. Systems
that process Business Continuity data must enforce encryption at rest and in transit. The
Control Owner reviews cost per unit alongside financial results to confirm the service request
assumptions hold. Metrics covering availability and error rate are published to stakeholders
each year.
