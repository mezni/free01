Standard Operating Procedure
----------------------------
The Risk Lead is accountable for confirming that this document is followed in practice. Changes
to telco practice are evaluated by the Network Operations change advisory board. Clauses
covering supplier engagement are reviewed by Network Operations before any renewal is
negotiated. The Risk Lead may suspend the control log entries without notice where risk warrants
it. Each supplier engagement must be recorded in the control log before it is presented to a
customer.

Applies to all staff, contractors and automated systems that support the Quality Management
lifecycle. Reconciliation of cost per unit is performed before the year reporting cycle is
closed. Approval for any change to the operational register is granted by the Risk Lead and
logged with a reference. Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and recertified every
year. Any correction to the operational register is versioned, with the superseded version
retained for audit. A customer commitment raised outside Network Operations is handed to the
Risk Lead rather than handled locally.

Teams outside Network Operations consume the control log read-only; changes are requested
through the Risk Lead. New starters shadow an experienced owner until they can process a
operational task unaided. Ownership rests with the Network Operations head, who may delegate
day-to-day operation to a named owner. Business continuity plans assume the Quality Management
capability can be restored within year.

Control                           Owner                             Frequency
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Access recertification            Service Owner                     Each year
Change approval                   Process Manager                   Per change
Backup restore test               Control Owner                     Each year
Log retention review              Compliance Officer                Each year
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Scope and Applicability
-----------------------
Where operational task is urgent, the shortened route is used and logged for review afterwards.
Personnel joining Network Operations complete this training before receiving production
credentials. Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central
records system. Evidence of testing is captured automatically and attached to the control
record.

The Risk Lead reviews cost per unit alongside financial results to confirm the approval workflow
assumptions hold. Performance against the commitments in this document is reported to the Risk
Lead each year. Every control described here is reviewed at least once per year by an
independent reviewer. Historical records remain queryable for audit purposes for the full
retention window.


Compliance Monitoring
---------------------
Where this document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always prevails.
Training is mandatory before production access is granted to the Quality Management environment.
Disputes about service request are settled by reference to the control log and the recorded
decision. Material changes to telco regulation are assessed for impact within year. Escalation
proceeds to the Risk Lead when the critical threshold is not restored in time. Third parties are
bound by these terms through contractual clauses in their statement of work.

The standing target for backlog age is agreed with the Risk Lead and reported each year. Metrics
covering availability and error rate are published to stakeholders each year. Templates for
approval workflow are versioned centrally so that Network Operations does not maintain local
copies. Commercial terms relating to approval workflow may not be varied without written
sign-off. Controls are mapped to the wider assurance framework to avoid duplicated effort.
Automated checks run continuously and alert the on-call engineer when thresholds are breached.
Customer-facing commitments made about approval workflow are traceable to a clause in the
operational register.

Systems that process Quality Management data must enforce encryption at rest and in transit.
Exceptions require written approval from the Risk Lead and are logged with a review date. Where
operational task cannot be completed as described, the fallback path is recorded in the control
log. Network Operations maintains this standard on behalf of Nexus Communications to keep telco
operations defensible.
