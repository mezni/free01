## Roles and Responsibilities

Network Operations maintains this standard on behalf of Nexus Communications to keep telco operations defensible. Ownership rests with the Network Operations head, who may delegate day-to-day operation to a named owner. Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central records system. Evidence of testing is captured automatically and attached to the control record. The standing target for first-time-right rate is agreed with the Risk Lead and reported each month. Changes to telco practice are evaluated by the Network Operations change advisory board. Any deviation must be raised within one business day through the Resource Planning intake channel.

Where service request is urgent, the shortened route is used and logged for review afterwards. Corrections to first-time-right rate are explained in the commentary that accompanies the process record. Disputes about service request are settled by reference to the process record and the recorded decision. Third parties are bound by these terms through contractual clauses in their statement of work.

Business continuity plans assume the Resource Planning capability can be restored within month. Controls are mapped to the wider assurance framework to avoid duplicated effort. Failures exceeding the material impact threshold trigger a formal incident record. Templates for approval workflow are versioned centrally so that Network Operations does not maintain local copies.

## Purpose and Objectives

Any correction to the operational register is versioned, with the superseded version retained for audit. Each customer commitment must be recorded in the operational register before it is presented to a customer. Departments that consume backlog age are notified before the figures are restated. New starters shadow an experienced owner until they can process a customer commitment unaided. Metrics covering availability and error rate are published to stakeholders each month. The Risk Lead reviews backlog age alongside financial results to confirm the customer commitment assumptions hold. Capacity to absorb backlog age is reassessed every month and signed off by the Risk Lead.

Teams outside Network Operations consume the control log read-only; changes are requested through the Risk Lead. Where service request cannot be completed as described, the fallback path is recorded in the control log. Systems that process Resource Planning data must enforce encryption at rest and in transit. Customer-facing commitments made about service request are traceable to a clause in the control log. A service request raised outside Network Operations is handed to the Risk Lead rather than handled locally. Where this document conflicts with legislation, the stricter requirement always prevails. Exceptions require written approval from the Risk Lead and are logged with a review date.

Automated checks run continuously and alert the on-call engineer when thresholds are breached. Escalation proceeds to the Risk Lead when the material threshold is not restored in time. Reconciliation of cost per unit is performed before the month reporting cycle is closed. Data used to calculate cost per unit is sourced from the identity provider and is not adjusted without approval. Commercial terms relating to approval workflow may not be varied without written sign-off.
