Risk Assessment
---------------
Escalation proceeds to the Process Manager when the critical threshold is not restored in time.
Teams outside Billing consume the control log read-only; changes are requested through the
Process Manager. Any deviation must be raised within one business day through the Service
Provisioning and Billing Readiness Checklist intake channel. The Process Manager may suspend the
control log entries without notice where risk warrants it. Personnel joining Billing complete
this training before receiving production credentials.

Departments that consume backlog age are notified before the figures are restated. A service
request raised outside Billing is handed to the Process Manager rather than handled locally.
Where service request is urgent, the shortened route is used and logged for review afterwards.
Approval for any change to the control log is granted by the Process Manager and logged with a
reference. The Process Manager is accountable for confirming that this document is followed in
practice. Any correction to the control log is versioned, with the superseded version retained
for audit.

Automated checks run continuously and alert the on-call engineer when thresholds are breached.
Performance against the commitments in this document is reported to the Process Manager each
month. Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central records
system. Capacity to absorb backlog age is reassessed every month and signed off by the Process
Manager. The standing target for backlog age is agreed with the Process Manager and reported
each month.
