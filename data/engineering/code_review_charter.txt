Records and Retention
---------------------
Evidence of compliance is retained for a minimum of seven years in the central records system.
Any deviation must be raised within one business day through the Code Review intake channel.
Disputes about approval workflow are settled by reference to the control log and the recorded
decision. The standing target for throughput is agreed with the Data Steward and reported each
six months. Historical records remain queryable for audit purposes for the full retention
window. The Data Steward reviews throughput alongside financial results to confirm the approval
workflow assumptions hold.

Metrics covering availability and error rate are published to stakeholders each six months.
Ownership rests with the Engineering head, who may delegate day-to-day operation to a named
owner. Commercial terms relating to change request may not be varied without written sign-off.
Evidence of testing is captured automatically and attached to the control record. Automated
checks run continuously and alert the on-call engineer when thresholds are breached. Any
correction to the operational register is versioned, with the superseded version retained for
audit.

Metric                            Target                            Escalation if missed
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Availability                      99.5%                             Two consecutive six months
Change lead time                  Five business days                Any P1 change
Evidence completeness             100%                              Any gap at audit
Training completion               100%                              Before access grant
