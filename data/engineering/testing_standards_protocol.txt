Roles and Responsibilities
--------------------------
Departments that consume cost per unit are notified before the figures are restated. Where
supplier engagement cannot be completed as described, the fallback path is recorded in the
process record. The standing target for cost per unit is agreed with the Process Manager and
reported each year. Exceptions require written approval from the Process Manager and are logged
with a review date. Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and recertified every year.

The Process Manager reviews first-time-right rate alongside financial results to confirm the
approval workflow assumptions hold. Reconciliation of first-time-right rate is performed before
the year reporting cycle is closed. Personnel joining Engineering complete this training before
receiving production credentials. The Process Manager is accountable for confirming that this
document is followed in practice. Ownership rests with the Engineering head, who may delegate
day-to-day operation to a named owner. Business continuity plans assume the Testing capability
can be restored within year. The Process Manager may suspend the operational register entries
without notice where risk warrants it.
